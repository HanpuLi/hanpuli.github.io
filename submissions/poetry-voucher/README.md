# Poetry Voucher submission desk

Checked on 26 September 2026. The official title is **Poetry Voucher**. The public work is at <https://hanpuli.github.io/poetry-voucher/>; the current source of the work is `content/poetry-voucher.json`, `content/poetry-voucher-app/` and `docs/poetry-voucher-publication.md`.

`opportunities.csv` is a dated research ledger, not an application or a representation that eligibility has been confirmed. `LIVING_TODO.md` holds project tasks and links to real external deadlines in the case deadline register. Recheck each official call immediately before submission.

Draft manuscripts, application answers, unpublished budget assumptions and contact details belong in `private/`, which is gitignored. The author's poems already appear on the public site, but a new assembled competition manuscript should not be exposed through this public repository without a separate publication decision. Keep the source texts intact and do not remove published poems to claim unpublished status.

Do not submit, pay, send a pitch, accept terms, list goods for sale or switch on real payments without the author's approval of the exact action and final material.
