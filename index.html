Profile

selected chronology

2013–16

電視與廣播主持——正定縣電視台、河北交通廣播；春晚直播觀眾逾五十萬。Live television and radio presenting.

2016

青年主持人大賽冠軍。First Prize, Young Presenter Competition.

2018–

商業、建築與紀實攝影。Commercial, architectural and documentary photography.

2018–19

URY 88.3FM 主持與製作。Presenter and producer, University Radio York.

2020

濟南——參與營救被強制關押於扭轉治療機構的跨性別女性，地面協調。On-ground coordination in the rescue of a transgender woman forcibly detained in a conversion-therapy facility.

2020–23

北京同志中心志願寫作者；武漢同志中心跨性別方向志願者。Volunteer writing, community support and emergency PEP access.

2024

場記、譯者與研究員——約克大學劇組；兩部作品入圍第十四屆澳門國際微電影節與金雞影展展映單元。Script supervisor, translator and researcher on two festival-finalist productions.
