Chinese website font subsets
===========================

hanpu-chinese-common.woff2 (Hanpu Chinese Common), hanpu-chinese.woff2
(Hanpu Chinese), and hanpu-cjk-gap.woff2 (Hanpu CJK Gap) are derived font
programs made from I.MingCP 8.10. All three are licensed under the IPA Font

Modifications and materials for further modification
----------------------------------------------------
The two Chinese faces cover the site's actual Traditional and Simplified
Chinese characters. The common face contains identity/navigation characters
so other language editions need not load the complete Chinese subset. The
small CJK gap face retains the previous seven-character fallback for Japanese.
Unused glyphs and hinting are removed, relevant OpenType layout and vertical
metrics are retained, and the fonts are compressed to WOFF2. Internal family,
full, unique and PostScript names are changed; copyright/licence metadata is
retained. The glyph outlines are not redrawn.

The character lists and metadata are available beside the fonts:
hanpu-chinese-common-characters.txt
hanpu-chinese-characters.txt
hanpu-cjk-gap-characters.txt
hanpu-chinese.meta.json

How to replace a subset with the unmodified original
----------------------------------------------------
1. Download the original I.MingCP-8.10.ttf from the upstream URL above and
   verify the supplied SHA-256. Keep its original internal name and bytes.
2. Place that unmodified file at assets/fonts/I.MingCP-8.10.ttf in your copy
   of the site, together with IPA_Font_License_Agreement_v1.0.txt.
3. In the corresponding @font-face declarations in assets/site.css and the
   Poetry Voucher stylesheet, replace the subset URL with
   /assets/fonts/I.MingCP-8.10.ttf and change format("woff2") to
   format("truetype") where present. CSS family aliases may stay unchanged;
   the original font's internal name must remain unchanged.
4. Preserve the Chinese unicode-range declarations to keep the existing Latin
   and Japanese font selection, or remove a range when intentionally using
   the full original beyond those characters. Reload the page.

The normal site loads only the small subsets. Providing these replacement
instructions does not require normal visitors to download the full font.
Further distributed derived fonts must comply with the same IPA agreement,
including distinct names, the required materials and replacement means.
