# 漢文訓点表示 / Kanbun visual reader

This directory is a standalone sandboxed visual reading component:
kanbun-render.js/.css (vertical typesetter), viewer.js/.css (frame
controller) and generated works/*.html. All code here is the site's own.

The generated works/*.html contain the Traditional Chinese poems, title
data and editorial kanbun annotations. Those literary texts are copyright
Hanpu Li; see the site's copyright statement in README.md.

All 29 classical texts have source-anchored character-level marks,
including explicit treatment of modern idioms. Their inclusion is not a
claim of specialist certification or historical manuscript transmission.
