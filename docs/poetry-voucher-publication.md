# Poetry Voucher: publication decisions

Author interview: 24 September 2026. This document records production decisions;
it is not an exhibition record, a report of audience research or a reconstruction
of intentions from an external review.

## The work and its public interface

Poetry Voucher offers Hanpu Li's existing poems by the piece through a fictional
shop. The poems were not written for the receipt format. The project places
individual, time-consuming writing in a familiar retail procedure: a catalogue,
base specifications, optional extras, quantities, checkout and a receipt.

The intended encounter is on paper. Early print trials exist, as reported by the
author; the current public digital specimens are not photographs of those trials.
The web shop makes the project accessible and supplies printable files, but is
not claimed to replace the physical encounter or prove that an exhibition has
occurred. Current output still needs physical calibration.

The public shop issues only catalogue works. Visitors choose a poem, original
script, typeface, size, translations and quantity; they do not rewrite the poem.
The former open-text Studio is retired. Its original renderer remains an internal
production and regression fixture. That shared code is not a second public offer.

A shop order has one receipt and one separately numbered voucher per copy. A
receipt can itself run over several pages. The two document types need not be in
a one-to-one relationship. Whether visitors should receive a continuous strip or
already-separated sheets remains a physical presentation question, not a behaviour
that the current separated PDFs can establish.

## Prices and configurations

PV3 remains unchanged. It counts letters/numbers, nonempty source lines and
stanzas, then rounds the base charge upward to a .99 step. It does not measure
writing time, effort, literary intensity or fair compensation. A longer text
need not move to another price step; source line breaks, not automatic printed
wrapping, are counted.

The pixel typeface is the base specification. Website typefaces are an upgrade,
not a separate poem and not a reason to make the base unreadable. Public sample
captions must identify whether the sample includes this upgrade and translations.

Published translations are selectable additions to the output. Their fixed price
is part of the shop's rule, not a measured paper or translation-labour invoice.
The poems and translations remain freely readable on the website. No money is
collected. The displayed VAT and payment details are fictional, not tax advice or
records of actual trading.

No change to the poems or literary translations is authorised by this production
pass. Relationships in the original collections remain available through source
links; interpretations proposed by an auditor are not added as authorial intent.

## Taking the work away

The author permits personal saving, personal printing and giving printed copies
to other people. This is not a new blanket licence for third-party publication,
adaptation or commercial reuse. No additional licence is inferred from the fact
that recipients' later behaviour cannot be controlled.

The receipt and poems have a complete textual counterpart on the order page and
in a self-contained HTML download. It includes the receipt's English till wording,
all issued text, quantities, amounts, fictional status and linked voucher numbers.
The image PDF still has no text layer; the HTML is an alternative, not a claim
that the PDF has become accessible.

Multi-page receipts and vouchers have continuation identities and page counts.
Single-page outputs do not acquire unnecessary continuation furniture. This work
adds no QR-code certificates, ownership claims, artificial scarcity or accounts.

## Records and re-rendering

A new order freezes its texts, selected translations, amounts, quantities, payment
scene and receipt display metadata. It also records a text-snapshot hash and the
public build fingerprint, including the renderer and font-file hashes.

The fingerprint describes build inputs, not a guarantee of identical browser
rasterisation. Keep the original downloaded file as the fixed record of that
output. Opening an old order with a different build is labelled re-rendering;
old orders without a recorded renderer are labelled as such. No historical build
or text version is invented retrospectively.

Retiring the Studio must not delete previously consented custom text or invalidate
frozen historical reader-edition orders. Such text cannot be newly purchased in
the author shop, and the author's personal-use permission must not be attached to
someone else's historical reader text.

The generated `publication-build.js` contains only explicitly selected public
source and font hashes. It must never include device identifiers, print-service
addresses, credentials, private files or audience records.

## Next material and reader work

First inspect and identify the existing prints without replacing them with newly
made copies. Then calibrate only the chosen base specimen, website-typeface and
English specimen, and a meaningful long-output boundary. Record the actual paper,
printable width, output scale, settings and matching file. Do not treat digital
clarity as evidence of physical legibility.

A small initial reader session should observe choosing, understanding the base
and upgraded outputs, taking the paper, reading and deciding what to do with it.
Do not prompt a theory of debt, value or gifting before observing. The possibility
of giving someone a poem was reported by the author as early reader feedback;
it is not a completed comparative experiment.

Fading is a possible material direction, not a demonstrated effect of these
particular prints. It is not a mandatory extra project or a reason to delay
completion indefinitely. A modest attended table can test the intended encounter
without being represented as a gallery exhibition.
