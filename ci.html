《臨江仙》

麴櫱鹽梅無舊譜，塵來未皺芳流。 簾前欲去影先留。 緣愁都不守，只道別家秋。 閬苑蓬萊身已遠，青驄且繫書樓。 別期未至不題愁。 炭冰摧過後，吟捻憶君眸。
二〇一八年一月八日至十四日作；二〇二六年十月修訂

To the tune of Linjiangxian

Ferment and malt, salt and plum: no old recipe; dust comes, but has not rippled the fragrant stream. Before the curtain, about to leave, the shadow stays first. I hold neither ties nor sorrow, speak only of your autumn. Far now from Langyuan and Penglai, tether the blue-grey horse awhile beside the study. The time for parting has not come; do not write sorrow yet. After charcoal and ice have torn through, I murmur verse, twist a whisker, remember your eyes.

《朝中措》

鷦金梳作暗香英，香到鏡中停。 偶影欄前欲並，一絲朱髮先縈。 唇邊鳳語，挹來便作，銀磬微鳴。 字底分明芒刺，唇間猶護餘聲。
二〇一八年十月二十三日作；二〇二六年十月修訂

To the tune of Chaozhongcuo

Feather-gold combed into a blossom of hidden scent; the scent reaches the mirror and stops. With only a shadow for company at the railing, longing to stand side by side; one strand of red hair entwines first. Phoenix words at the lips: once drawn in, they become the faint ringing of a silver chime-stone. Beneath the words, the thorns are clear; between the lips, the lingering sound is sheltered still.

《風入松》

丘亭欲雪已凝霜，依舊頑陽。 斜暉落甓猶難去，然金爐、待子宵長。 千里行來至此，雁丘今可成堂。 舊山橫影亂爐光，燼處餘香。 筆枯化紙容人念，料經年、應易遷忘。 芍藥更添紅雨，玉筋暗觸龍章。
二〇一八年十一月二日作；二〇二六年十月修訂

To the tune of Fengrusong

At the hill pavilion, snow is about to fall; frost has already formed, yet the stubborn sun remains. Slanting light falls on the bricks and still will not depart; the golden brazier is lit, waiting for you through the long night. A thousand li travelled to this point; Wild Goose Mound may now become a hall. The old mountain’s crossing shadow disturbs the brazier light; among the embers, fragrance remains. The brush runs dry; paper is given to the flames, yet still holds a person’s thoughts. I suppose that, with the years, forgetting should come easily. The peony adds still more red rain; jade sinew secretly touches dragon splendour.

《鵲橋仙》

未消尺素，自珍故物，紙上尋常字句。 柔荑傷處竟唇濡，經年後、還留幾許。 聞人道好，隨人道好，瓦釜雷鳴何故。 逢君仍問近來安，說罷了、回身讓路。
二〇一八年十二月二十二日作；二〇二六年十月修訂

To the tune of Queqiaoxian

No need for letters; I treasure the old things. On the paper, only ordinary words. Yet lips moistened the wound on a tender hand; years later, how much remains? Hearing others call it good, I call it good too. Why does the earthen pot thunder? Meeting you, I still ask how you have been; when the words are done, I turn and make way.

《臨江仙引》

塢裂，宴散，鐘垂晚、暫偷安。 星師不復嘉年。 任舊牆吞影，剩歸夢盤桓。 空庭雪柳，折處尚溫，偏倚爛柯山。 三山別來無去處，苔階不認人還。 度鐵城礄路，更麻雨潺潺。 拋殘血縷滿指，待來世兩相憐。
二〇一九年十月二十八日作；二〇二六年十月修訂

To the tune of Linjiangxian Yin

The enclosure cracks; the feast disperses; the bell hangs into evening—a moment’s stolen peace. Star City no longer holds those good years. Let the old wall swallow the shadow; only the homeward dream circles on. Snow-willow in the empty courtyard, still warm at the break, yet leaning against Mount Lanke. Since leaving the Three Mountains, nowhere to go; the mossy steps do not recognise the one returning. Through iron city, along stony road, with hemp-rain murmuring on. Cast-off blood-threads still cover the fingers; may we cherish each other in another life.

《滿庭芳 · 遙思楚潭》

風佩初停，雨梳朝蕣，尺書先識眉彎。 一聲低喚，驚散夢中弦。 墨跡新添氣息，微微顫、欲出行間。 相逢處，餘溫未定，人近楚天寬。 辭顏。名已熟，臨唇卻緩，舌底生瀾。 把千里關山，盡斂襟前。 幾月光陰忽薄，回眸際、指過眉山。 從頭認，詩還半闋，雙影壓箋邊。
二〇一九年十二月十九日作；二〇二六年十月修訂

To the tune of Mantingfang · Thinking of Chutan from afar

The wind’s pendants have just stilled; rain combs the morning hibiscus; letters knew the arch of the brows first. One low call scatters the dream’s strings. Fresh breath enters the ink-traces; faintly trembling, they are about to emerge from between the lines. At our meeting, the lingering warmth has yet to settle; with you near, the sky of Chu grows wide. Ciyan. A familiar name, yet slow upon the lips; waves rise beneath the tongue. A thousand li of mountains and passes, all gathered at the breast. Months of time grow suddenly thin; in a backward glance, a finger passes over the mountain of a brow. To know you again, from the beginning; the poem still half-written, two shadows press on the edge of the page.

《滿江紅 · 望沮攔約當應是》

舊隙才縫，春風又、殷勤留客。 門欲掩、卻翻簾影，放春偷入。 瘦損腰圍衣尚窄，暗痕都向肌膚積。 算歸來、猶有舊針留，縫新隙。 燈未冷，人已易。門不閉，風偏急。 更何須揀擇，過門皆璧。 初景寒柯違業識，豐城元禮宜同席。 看收場、針腳又周全，無痕跡。
二〇二二年原作；二〇二六年十月重寫（縫補稿）

To the tune of Manjianghong · Hoping to hold you back, knowing how it must go — I

The old split barely sewn, spring wind again presses a guest to stay. The door would close; the curtain’s shadow turns instead, letting spring slip in. The waist has grown thin, yet the garment is still tight; hidden marks all gather on the skin. On the return, I reckon, the old needle will still be there, to sew the new split. The lamp has not cooled; already someone else. The door stays open; the wind grows urgent. Why bother choosing? Everyone through the door is jade. First light and the cold branch run counter in karmic consciousness; Fengcheng and Yuanli ought to sit together. See how it ends: the stitching whole again, no trace.

《滿江紅 · 望沮攔約當應是 · 又稿》

漏盡鐘回，催曉處、餘聲仍塞。 門欲掩、劣衿將去，又迎嘉客。 舊事還從新曆出，階前風雨渾成笛。 算韶光、只在一聲中，塵先積。 空空雪，留故色。光易轉，痕難拭。 縱衣冠屢換，總稱圭璧。 初景寒柯違業識，豐城元禮宜同席。 曉山塗，晝影祭寒華，猶無息。
二〇二二年原作；二〇二六年十月重寫（又稿）

To the tune of Manjianghong · Hoping to hold you back, knowing how it must go — another version

The water-clock runs out; the bell returns. Where it urges dawn, its lingering sound is still obstructed. The door would close; the shabby collar is about to leave, yet another fine guest is welcomed. Old events come out of the new calendar again; before the steps, wind and rain turn wholly to flute-song. All that fair time, within a single sound—and dust has gathered first. Empty, empty snow keeps the old colour. Light turns easily; the trace is hard to wipe away. However often robes and caps are changed, all are called precious jade. First light and the cold branch run counter in karmic consciousness; Fengcheng and Yuanli ought to sit together. Dawn’s mountain is clogged; daylight’s shadow makes offerings to cold bloom, still without cease.

A1 · 甲一《臨江仙 · 與同硯燈下夜坐，侵曉方別》

幾度深宵同默坐，孤燈紅淚垂遲。 一秋心緒有誰知。 啓窗星欲墜，光落兩人衣。 不語各含無限思，而今惟道相期。 夜闌暗忘漏聲移。 慵來還指日，明日又今時。

To the tune of Linjiangxian · Sitting with a fellow student by lamplight at night; parting only at dawn

How often we sat together silent, deep into the night; the lone lamp’s red tears slow to fall. Who is there to know the feelings of a whole autumn? We open the window: a star about to fall, its light upon both our clothes. Silent, each holding thoughts without end; now we say only that we will meet again. The night grows late; unnoticed, the water-clock moves on. Languid now, we still name a day: tomorrow, at this same hour.

B1 · 乙一《無題 · 臨江仙》

虛廊月好人初定，同攜花徑遲遲。 燈前笑語兩無疑。 舊衾隨手與，今日不須知。 客散非因誰獨在，他人皆自相依。 席旁偶坐共相棲。 漏殘晨露上，風過落花時。

Untitled · To the tune of Linjiangxian

The moon is bright in the empty corridor; people have only just settled. Together we take our time along the flowered path. By the lamp we laugh and talk, neither of us in doubt. The old quilt, handed over casually. Today, there is no need to know. The guests disperse, but not because anyone is left alone; the others each have someone of their own to lean on. By chance we sit beside the table, and there we roost together. The water-clock runs low; morning dew gathers. Wind passes, as petals fall.

A2 · 甲二《臨江仙 · 醉後扶靨登樓，夤夜同倚，人問不避》

沈醉曾扶相並倚，登樓未肯人知。 今宵燈影漸迷離。 漏殘催曙色，猶帶靨邊緋。 同擁寒衾還細話，當筵曾許心期。 醒來可認醉中辭？ 朝霞渾未記，宿霧已盈墀。

To the tune of Linjiangxian · After wine, cheek supported as we climbed upstairs; leaning together far into the night; when people asked, not evading them

Deep in drink, we steadied one another and leaned side by side. We went upstairs, unwilling to let others know. Tonight the lamplight grows indistinct. The water-clock runs low, hastening the dawn; a flush still lingers at the dimpled cheek. Together under the cold quilt, still talking softly; at the feast, a promise of the heart was once made. Awake, will what was said in drink still be acknowledged? I remember nothing of the morning’s rosy light. Night mist has already filled the steps.

B2 · 乙二《無題 · 踏莎行》

一室香温，兩痕影並。 笑聲沉入簾間暝。 隔簾燈色欲先寒，有人暗處衣光冷。 欲喚還休，歡猶未省。 分明一水無舟艇。 殘灰散盡各歸時，餘釭獨照空庭靜。

Untitled · To the tune of Tasuoxing

A room warm with scent; two traces of shadow side by side. Laughter sinks into the dark between the curtains. Beyond the curtain, even the lamplight seems the first to chill; someone in the dark, the light on their clothes grown cold. About to call out, then holding back; the beloved still unaware. One stretch of water, plain to see. No boat. As the last ash scatters and each goes home, the remaining lamp alone lights the still, empty courtyard.

B3 · 乙三《無題 · 臨江仙》

燭暗蛩寒簾影瘦，殘酲猶帶微温。 筆停紙澀墨先吞。 未緘還自疊，一折一重門。 不是東風先折取，恐花為我辭根。 新痕舊鑰莫輕論。 相逢原是客，客受主家恩。

Untitled · To the tune of Linjiangxian

Candle dim, crickets cold, the curtain’s shadow thin; the wine’s last haze still holds a little warmth. The brush stops. The paper resists. The ink swallows first. Still unsealed, I fold it up again; with each fold, one more door. It is not that the east wind broke it off first; I fear the flower may leave its roots for me. Do not speak lightly of new marks and an old key. When we met, I was only ever a guest; and a guest receives the kindness of the house.

B4 · 乙四《無題 · 渡江雲》

清銅新樣齒，欲封還止，殘熱指間彈。 忽燈前小影，緘淺香微，半晌已潸然。 綢繆一晌，轉覺是、別處温言。 空鑄得、舊模新鑰，紋疊總相沿。 更闌。 啼痕自拭，燭燼重挑，奈宵長箋短。 謄復塗、深言怕誤，淺字偏安。 明朝銅色經他手，便認作、一諾窮年。 燈滅後、丁寧只是加餐。

Untitled · To the tune of Dujiangyun

Bright copper, teeth newly fashioned. About to seal it, I stop; still warm, it is flicked between my fingers. Suddenly, a small shadow before the lamp; the letter lightly sealed, its scent faint; and in a moment the tears have come. A spell of tenderness; then the sense that these are warm words belonging elsewhere. In vain I have cast a new key from the old mould; the grooves, layer on layer, all follow the same lines. The night wears late. I wipe away the tears, trim the wick again. What can be done: the night so long, the paper so short. Copy out, cross out again; I fear the deeper words may be misread; plainer words stay safe. Tomorrow, when the copper passes into the other’s hands, it will be taken for a promise good for a lifetime. After the lamp is out, all I urge is: eat well.

B5 · 乙五《無題 · 十六字令》

銅。鑰齒新裁付掌中。天寒了，加飯且從容。

Untitled · To the tune of Shiliuziling

Copper. A key, teeth newly cut, placed in your palm. The cold has come. Eat well, and take your time.

A3 · 甲三《鷓鴣天 · 卿贈鑰，附書數字，燈下展讀》

鑰齒新裁心自珍，提毫未寫已逡巡。 拆來只有三行字，半晌綢繆一晌嗔。 星欲墜，露初勻。 當時未敢喚真真。 案前字暖春封淺，猶有殘香繞指温。

To the tune of Zhegutian · You gave me a key, with a note of a few words enclosed; unfolded and read by lamplight

Teeth of the key newly cut; I treasure it. I lift the brush; before a word is written, already I hesitate. On opening it, only three lines of writing. A while of tenderness, a spell of pique. A star about to fall; the dew just settling evenly. Back then I dared not call out to Zhenzhen in the painting. Warm words on the desk, the spring letter lightly sealed; a lingering scent still curls warm round my fingers.

A4 · 甲四，憶甲一《臨江仙 · 曩與同硯默坐數夕，別後音塵寖薄，久乃自定》

曾共虛廊默坐，當時只道尋常。 孤燈紅淚共商量。 不知秋已半，但覺夜初長。 別後音書都杳，逢人怕説西窗。 偶因殘夢溯流光。 思忘還入枕，避跡反成雙。

To the tune of Linjiangxian · Recalling A1: once I sat silent for several evenings with a fellow student; after parting, news grew scarce, and only after a long while did I settle myself

Once we sat together silent in the empty corridor; back then I thought it only ordinary. The lone lamp’s red tears joined in our counsel. I did not know autumn was half gone; I only felt the nights begin to lengthen. Since parting, letters and news have vanished. Meeting people, I am afraid to speak of the west window. Now and then a broken dream carries me back upstream through the years. What I would forget comes back with me to the pillow; shunning every trace, I find myself paired with it still.

A5 · 甲五，憶甲二《臨江仙 · 記醉後扶靨登樓，夤夜人問，始無避焉》

那夜誰分賓主，並肩同倚更闌。 當筵人問不曾瞞。 雪泥誰去早，此後各風煙。 別處星辰未改，人間幾換流年。 舊潮猶帶醉時顏。 思忘成此闋，相對鬢先斑。

To the tune of Linjiangxian · Recalling A2: after wine, cheek supported as we climbed upstairs; when people asked late that night, for the first time nothing was evaded

That night, who distinguished guest from host? Shoulder to shoulder, leaning together as the watch ran late. At the feast, when people asked, I did not conceal it. On the snow and mud, who was first to leave? From then on, each in our own wind and mist. Elsewhere the stars have not changed; down in the world, how many years have turned over. The old tide still carries a face flushed with wine. The wish to forget has become this lyric; face to face, our temples are the first to grey.

A6 · 甲六，憶甲三《鷓鴣天 · 篋中故鑰銅花滿眼，追念往還書字，悵然有作》

小鑰經年鏽暗生，故箋翻罷眼猶明。 當時避卻鴛鴦字，卻把鴛鴦寫幾層。 窗月冷，鬢霜驚。 如今畫裏喚誰名。 相看似隔前生事，齧盡痴根草又青。

To the tune of Zhegutian · Recalling A3: the old key in the box, covered in verdigris; thinking back over the letters exchanged, I wrote this in melancholy

A small key: with the years, rust has darkly grown. The old letters read through, my eyes are still bright. Back then, the words “mandarin ducks” were left unwritten; yet mandarin ducks were written in, layer upon layer. Moon cold at the window; frost at the temples, a shock. Now, in the painting, whose name is there to call? Facing one another, as though across a former life. The root of longing gnawed away to nothing; the grass is green again.

A7 · 甲七《臨江仙 · 分蕙寄某，憶昔贈蘭蓀時，惘然有作》

手分舊蕙移新種，如今可記春温。 當初玉指贈蘭蓀。 不知根已定，空自換苔痕。 別後西窗青幾度，也應灌溉朝昏。 爾來各自長年輪。 偶然風過處，依約暗香聞。

To the tune of Linjiangxian · Dividing an orchid to send to someone; remembering the time the fragrant herbs were given; written in wistfulness

By hand I divide the old orchid for new plantings. Now, is the warmth of that spring still remembered? Back then, jade fingers gave the fragrant herbs. I did not know the roots had already taken hold; only the traces of moss kept changing, to no purpose. Since parting, how many times has the west window turned green? You too, I suppose, water at dawn and dusk. Since then, each of us has grown our own rings of years. Where a wind happens to pass, a faint scent, just discernible.

B6 · 乙六《得某所寄》

故盆移蕙草，幾度誤春痕。 鄰圃分新種，行人折故根。 窗虛留寸土，門掩各朝昏。 別後遷新邑，經年異故園。 囊空行愈遠，塵積逐年增。 盆碎無遺瓦，殘根失所憑。 啟緘留指印，手尚帶春泥。 稚女描新帖，盆花落漸稀。 虛廊月正好，燈下話綢繆。 君書千里至，字字是舊秋。 展卷心先遠，擱筆無可酬。 非是無一字，風過落花休。 掩卷天將暮，稚女索殘詩。 舊盆花事盡，春至又生枝。 澆水成前事，素心終不移。 君舟向滄海，我亦有天涯。

On receiving what was sent

In the old pot, the orchid transplanted; more than once, spring’s trace went awry. The neighbouring garden takes a new division; a passer-by breaks off an old root. At the empty window, an inch of earth remains; behind the closed door, each to our own dawns and dusks. After we parted, I moved to a new town; years passed; nothing like the old garden. With an empty purse I go farther still; dust accumulates with every year. The pot breaks, leaving not one shard; the roots that remain have nothing left to hold. I unseal the letter, leaving a finger-mark; spring mud still on my hands. My little daughter copies from a fresh writing model; the potted blossoms fall and thin. The moon was bright in the empty corridor; by the lamp we spoke in tenderness. Your letter has come a thousand li; every word an autumn long ago. I open the pages, my heart already far away; I set down the brush; there is no answering in kind. It is not that I have nothing to say; wind passes; fallen blossoms rest. I close the pages; dusk is coming. My little daughter asks for the leftover verses. In the old pot the flowers are over; spring comes, and shoots grow again. The watering is a thing of the past; the plain heart, after all, has not changed. Your boat heads for the open sea; I too have a horizon of my own.

A8 · 甲八《夜聞舊曲》

當時窗下別，今作檐前雨。 怕聽舊時曲，聲聲催天曙。 餘音不肯絕，繞作迴腸縷。 一燈寒在壁，照壁如照汝。 昔我今何在，孤影各茫然。 推窗山似海，萬古一微弦。 此聲殊未已，人世不知年。 側耳疑相答，餘音何處傳。

Hearing an old tune at night

Then, a parting beneath the window; now, rain before the eaves. I fear to hear the old tune; note after note hastens the dawn. The lingering sound will not break off, winding into threads that twist the heart. A lone lamp, cold against the wall; lighting the wall as though lighting you. Where is the self I was, now? Lone shadows, each at a loss. I push the window open: mountains like a sea; all the ages, one faint string. This sound has still not ceased; here in the world, no reckoning of years. I turn an ear, unsure if something answers. Where is the lingering sound carried?

B7 · 乙七《忘川 · 檢篋得舊稿，不復攜，悉以寄某，並題》

燈下讀舊詞，夜深方得眠。 當時渾不異，往事付流年。 我又移新處，君猶理舊篇。 小兒催入夢，蕙草立窗前。 莫攀檐前柳，莫數落花天。 忘川人自渡，各自有歸邊。

Wangchuan, the river of forgetting · Searching the box, I found old drafts; rather than carry them further, I sent them all, with these lines

By lamplight I read the old poems; only deep in the night could I sleep. Back then, none of it seemed any different; what has passed I give over to the years. I have moved somewhere new again; you still put the old pages in order. The child hurries me towards sleep; the orchid stands before the window. Do not reach for the willow before the eaves. Do not count the days of falling petals. Each crosses the river of forgetting alone; each has a shore to come home to.

A9 · 甲九《鷓鴣天 · 得某所寄舊稿，燈下讀竟，與篋中所存合編，第其甲乙。裝成，客至輒出以相示，題其尾》

兩疊殘箋展案前，舊時燈影各成篇。 親將一線縫卿我，留得鴛鴦傍脊眠。 賓一哂，語猶慳。 箋中淺字只加餐。 深言怕誤重重掩，欲喚還休欲喚難。

To the tune of Zhegutian · On receiving the old drafts sent to me, I read them by lamplight, combined them with those kept in my box, and arranged them as A and B. Once bound, I brought them out whenever a guest called, and wrote this at the end

Two sheaves of old pages spread before me on the desk; in the lamplight of those days, each had formed a poem of its own. With my own hands, I stitch you and me with a single thread; and leave the mandarin ducks to sleep beside the spine. A guest smiles; speech stays spare. The plainer words on the page say only: eat well. Deeper words, for fear they may be misread, lie veiled layer upon layer; about to call out, then holding back; about to call out is hard.

A10 · 集外 · 甲十《中呂 · 山坡羊 · 又過舊廬》

風迎面，人立此， 鼻尖一動魂先至。 認不真，卻認真， 繞來繞去街角立。 廿年風霜渾不記， 這點子氣味偏惦記。 遲，是誤了； 歸，也是誤了。

Outside the cycle · Zhonglü · To the tune of Shanpoyang · Passing the old house again

Wind in the face. Stopped here. The nose moves first — the soul arrives before I do. Can’t be sure it’s real; am sure anyway. Round and round the corner of the street, and standing still. Twenty years of weather, none of it kept — this little bit of smell, kept. Too late — that was the mistake. Coming back — that was the mistake too.

《鷓鴣天 · 連夕燈下試筆，未竟》

幾夕簾前共剪燈，平生話到又三更。 從頭説盡還存半，且任宵深硯自凝。 言漸杳，露初零。獨餘一語未分明。 頻頻蘸得毫端黑，墨至濃時偏不成。
二〇二六年九月九日

To the tune of Zhegutian · Several evenings at the lamp, trying to write; unfinished

Some nights, before the curtain, we trimmed the lamp together; a whole life talked through, and again it was the third watch. Told from the beginning, told to the end — half of it still there. Let the night go deep; let the ink congeal in the inkstone by itself. The words thin out. The dew begins to fall. One phrase alone is left, and it will not come clear. Again and again the brush-tip comes up black; when the ink is thickest, it will not make the words.

《臨江仙》

簾影幾宵長共話，露痕初濕闌干。 言猶未了息先安。 夢深渾不覺，月上夜將殘。 他日重簾垂到地，垂來一似從前。 手臨燈畔數迴旋。 明朝風或緊，窗且掩周全。
二〇二六年九月九日

To the tune of Linjiangxian

Shadow of the curtain, several nights of long talk; the first dew wetting the rail. The words not finished — the breathing settled first. Deep in dream, knowing nothing; the moon up, the night nearly gone. Some other day the heavy curtain will fall to the floor, fall exactly as it did before. A hand beside the lamp, turning a few times. Tomorrow the wind may blow hard; for now, shut the window well.

A later response

An independent poem written in October 2026, looking back on the poems of 2022.

  1. To the tune of Manjianghong · Rereading an old poem, remembering contact renewed after years

《滿江紅 · 重讀舊作，憶經年復通音問事》

舊闋重翻，先認得、當年心力。 曾細剖、片言千轉，氣微聲澀。 鎖盡諸門身作鎖，翻來私語都成棘。 縱丁寧、舌底已春深，花時隔。 名復見，箋乍濕。言可續，時難逆。 任音書又杳，不催消息。 字入年輪方有響，痕藏木理猶能識。 聽鐘回、晝影過寒柯，尋常日。
二〇二六年十月另作

To the tune of Manjianghong · Rereading an old poem, remembering contact renewed after years

Turning the old verses again, I first recognise the strength I spent then. I once took it all apart so finely: a few words turned a thousand times, breath faint and voice rough. Locking every door, I made my body a lock; private words, turned over, all became thorns. For all my earnest urging, spring was deep beneath my tongue, yet the flowering time lay apart. The name appears again; the page is suddenly wet. Words can resume; time is hard to turn back. Let word fall silent again; I do not press for news. Only when words enter the growth-rings do they acquire resonance; the mark hidden in the grain can still be read. I hear the bell return; daylight’s shadow crosses the cold branch. An ordinary day.
