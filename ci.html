甲乙十六首

一人分作两声的唱和集：甲详序，乙无题。十六首并系于二〇二六年六月九日；集外一首，七月一日。附自撰笺注，备索。
A poem cycle written in two voices by one hand — 甲 keeps prefaces, 乙 refuses titles. The sixteen poems are dated 9 June 2026; the one outside the cycle, 1 July. With the author’s own philological apparatus, available on request.

甲一《临江仙 · 与同砚灯下夜坐，侵晓方别》

几度深宵同默坐，孤灯红泪垂迟。 一秋心绪有谁知。 启窗星欲坠，光落两人衣。 不语各含无限思，而今惟道相期。 夜阑暗忘漏声移。 慵来还指日，明日又今时。
After Linjiangxian · Night sitting with a schoolfriend; we did not part till dawnSo many nights we sat in the deep hours saying nothing, the lamp crying its red tears at its own pace. Who was to know what a whole autumn had gathered. When we opened the window a star was about to fall. Its light came down on both our sleeves at once. Each of us full past speaking; all we ever said was: next time. Dark grew late; the water-clock moved on unheard. Half asleep, we still set the day — tomorrow. This same hour.

乙一《无题 · 临江仙》

虚廊月好人初定，同携花径迟迟。 灯前笑语两無疑。 旧衾随手与，今日不须知。 客散非因谁独在，他人皆自随依。 席旁偶坐共相栖。 漏残晨露上，风过落花时。
Untitled · After LinjiangxianGood moon on the empty corridor, the house just settling. We took the flowered path slowly, since it was there. Lamplight, laughter, neither of us doubting anything. The old quilt changed hands because it was in reach. Today there is nothing that needs knowing. The guests dispersed — not that anyone stayed on; people leave in ones and twos, leaning on their own. We happened to be seated where we perched. The clock ran down. Dew rose toward morning. Wind passed; it was the hour petals go.

甲二《临江仙 · 醉后扶靥登楼，夤夜同倚，人问不避》

沈醉曾扶相并倚，登楼未肯人知。 今宵灯影渐迷离。 更残催曙色，犹带靥边绯。 同拥寒衾还细话，当筵曾许心期。 那时颦笑欲询谁。 朝霞浑未记，宿雾已盈墀。
After Linjiangxian · Helping a flushed face up the stairs; leaning together far into the night; when they asked, we did not hideDeep in the wine, I steadied you; we leaned as one. Climbing, we let no one know. Tonight the lamplight blurs and doubles. The watch runs down, dawn pressing at the color still warm along your cheek. One quilt against the cold, still talking low; at the open table we promised what hearts promise. That brow, that laughter — and no one now to ask. Of the red morning I remember nothing. Night mist already stood upon the steps.

乙二《无题 · 踏莎行》

一室香温，两痕影并。 笑声沉向欢边暝。 隔帘灯色欲先寒，有人暗处衣光冷。 欲唤还休，前欢未省。 分明一水无舟艇。 残灰散尽各归时，余釭独照空庭静。
Untitled · After TasuoxingOne room warm with scent; two shadows printed side by side. Laughter sinking toward the dark where the dear one sits. Through the curtain even the lamp goes cold first; someone in the shadows, the light along their clothes gone cold. About to call out; letting it go. The one in joy noticed nothing. Plainly, a single stretch of water. No boat. When the last ash scattered and each went home, one lamp was left to light the empty courtyard, still.

乙三《无题 · 临江仙》

烛暗蛩寒帘影瘦，残酲阁角微温。 笔停纸涩墨先吞。 未缄还自叠，心事付黄昏。 不是东风先折取，恐花为我辞根。 新痕旧钥莫轻论。 相逢原是客，客作主家恩。
Untitled · After LinjiangxianCandle low, crickets cold, the curtain’s shadow thinning; last of the wine still warm in a corner of the room. The pen stops. The paper resists. The ink swallows first. Unsealed, folded again and again by hand — the heart’s business handed over to the dusk. It isn’t that the east wind broke the flower first; the fear is that the flower left its root for me. New marks, old key — neither to be spoken of lightly. We met as passing guests; the guest lives on the kindness of the house.

乙四《无题 · 渡江云》

清铜新样齿，欲封还止，残热指间弹。 忽灯前小影，缄浅香微，半晌已潸然。 绸缪一晌，转觉是、别处温言。 空覆得、旧模新钥，纹叠总相沿。 更阑。 啼痕自拭，烛烬重挑，夜长笺纸短。 誊复涂、深言怕误，浅字偏安。 明朝铜色经他手，便认作、一诺穷年。 灯灭后、丁宁只是加餐。
Untitled · After DujiangyunBright copper, teeth new-cut. About to seal it; stopping. What heat is left flicked from between the fingers. Then, small in the lamplight, a shadow of a self; the envelope thin, the scent faint — a long moment, and the tears had come. One spell of tenderness, and it turns out to be warm words that belong elsewhere. All I have pressed is a new key from an old mould, the grain repeating, layer on layer. The night wears down. Wipe the crying clean. Trim the wick again. The night is long; the paper is short. Copy, cross out — the deep words fear their errors; the shallow words sit safe. Tomorrow the copper will pass through another hand and be taken for a promise good for years. After the lamp is out, all I dare urge is: eat well.

乙五《无题 · 十六字令》

铜。钥齿新裁付掌中。天寒了，加饭且从容。
Untitled · After ShiliuzilingCopper. A key, teeth fresh from the cutting, closed into a palm. The cold’s come. Eat well, and take your time.

甲三《鹧鸪天 · 卿赠钥，附书数字，灯下展读》

钥齿新裁心自珍，欲题书字语难申。 拆开只有三行字，半晌绸缪一晌嗔。 星欲坠，露初匀。当时未敢唤真真。 案前字暖春封浅，犹有残香绕指温。
After Zhegutian · A key sent, with a few written lines; opened and read by lamplightTeeth of the key new-cut; I kept it dear. Wanting to write back — the words would not be said. Opened: three lines of writing, only three. A long while tender; one short while cross. A star about to fall; the dew just evening out. I dared not, then, call the picture true. The words lay warm on the desk, the seal light with spring; some scent still going warm around the finger.

甲四，忆甲一《临江仙 · 曩与同砚默坐数夕，别后音尘寖薄，久乃自定》

曾共虚廊默坐，当时只道寻常。 孤灯红泪共商量。 不知秋已半，但觉夜初长。 别后音书都杳，逢人怕说西窗。 偶因残梦溯流光。 思忘还入枕，避迹反成双。
After Linjiangxian · Remembering: nights of sitting silent; word grew thin after parting; in time I settledOnce we sat in the empty corridor saying nothing, and I called it ordinary, then. One lamp, a clear shadow laid on the west window. Not knowing autumn was half gone — only that night was newly long. Since parting, not a line, not a sound. Meeting people, I am afraid to speak of the west window. Now and then a broken dream goes back upstream. What I would forget follows me into the pillow; what I avoid, by avoiding, comes in pairs.

甲五，忆甲二《临江仙 · 记醉后扶靥登楼，夤夜人问，始无避焉》

曾倚微醺乘月，并肩同倚更阑。 当筵人问不曾瞒。 雪泥谁去早，从此各风烟。 别处星辰未改，人间几换流年。 旧潮犹带醉时颜。 思忘偏又记，相对鬓先斑。
After Linjiangxian · Remembering: the flushed climb, the leaning late; when they asked that night, at last we did not hideWe leaned on a little wine and took the moon, shoulder to shoulder while the watch ran late. At the open table, asked, I did not lie. Whose prints left the snow first? After that, each our own smoke and weather. Elsewhere the stars have not moved on; down here the years have changed hands more than once. An old tide still carries the face of that drunk hour. What I meant to forget insists on being kept — and face to face, the temples gray before the rest.

甲六，忆甲三《鹧鸪天 · 箧中故钥铜花满眼，追念往还书字，怅然有作》

小钥经年锈暗生，故笺翻罢眼犹明。 当时避却鸳鸯字，却把鸳鸯写几层。 窗月冷，鬓霜惊。如今画里唤谁名。 相看似隔前生事，啮尽痴根草又青。
After Zhegutian · The old key flowered with rust; going back over the letters, desolate, I wrote thisA small key, years on, gone dark with bloom of rust; the old letters read again — the eyes still clear. We kept, back then, from writing mandarin ducks, and wrote the ducks in, layer under layer. Moon cold at the window; frost at the temples, sudden. Into the picture now, what name is there to call? We look at one another as across a former life. The root of longing, gnawed to nothing, greens again.

甲七《临江仙 · 分蕙寄某，忆昔赠兰荪时，惘然有作》

手分旧蕙移新种，凭君可记春温。 那年玉指赠兰荪。 不知根已定，空自换苔痕。 一别西窗青遍后，也应灌溉朝昏。 尔来各自长霜文。 偶然风过处，依约暗香闻。
After Linjiangxian · Dividing an orchid to send; remembering the year the sweet-grass was givenBy hand I split the old orchid for new soil. Do you remember the warmth of that spring? That year, jade fingers passed the sweet-grass over. I did not know the root had taken hold; only the moss changed, and changed again. Since the west window went all green without us, you too must water yours at dawn and dusk. Both of us, since, have grown our lines of frost. And where the wind happens through — dimly, the old scent again.

乙六《得某所寄》

故盆移蕙草，几度误春痕。 邻圃分新种，行人折故根。 窗虚留寸土，门掩各朝昏。 别后迁新邑，经年异故园。 囊空随路减，尘积逐年增。 盆碎无遗瓦，茎枯失旧藤。 君书今日至，手尚带春泥。 稚女描新帖，盆花落渐稀。 虚廊月正好，灯下话绸缪。 窗前蕙草绿，檐外晨露稠。 更残人未散，深意眼中收。 君书千里至，字字是旧秋。 展卷心先远，搁笔无可酬。 非是无一字，风过落花休。 掩卷天将暮，稚女索残诗。 空盆花事尽，春至又生枝。 浇水成前事，素心终不移。 君舟自沧海，我亦各天涯。
On receiving what was sentThe old pot’s orchid moved, and moved again; more than one spring went wrong in the marks it left. The next-door garden took a new division; passers-by broke what was left of the root. A spare inch of earth stayed under the window; the door shut on its own dawns and dusks. After the parting I moved to a new town; year by year it grew unlike the old garden. The bag grew lighter with every road; the dust thickened with every year. The pot broke and left no shard worth keeping; the stem dried and lost its old vine. Your letter came today, your hand still carrying spring mud. My small daughter traces her copybook; the flowers in the pot thin as they fall. The empty corridor, the moon at its best — lamplight, and the old tenderness talked over. Orchid-grass green at the window, morning dew thick past the eaves. The watch ran down; nobody rose to go; what was meant was gathered up by eye. Your letter crossed a thousand li; every word in it an old autumn. I opened it, and the heart went far ahead; I set down the pen with nothing to repay. Not that there was no word at all to send — the wind went through; the petals called it done. I closed the book toward evening; the child asked for the leftover poem. The empty pot is done with flowering; spring comes, and there are branches again. Watering it belongs to a former life. The plain heart, all the same, has never moved. Your boat is out of the wide sea; I too — each of us at our own end of the sky.

甲八《夜闻旧曲》

当时窗下别，今作檐前雨。 怕听旧时曲，声声催天曙。 余音不肯绝，绕作回肠缕。 一灯寒在壁，照壁如照汝。 昔我今何在，孤影各茫然。 推窗山似海，万古一微弦。 此声殊未已，人世不知年。 似响疑相答，馀音何处传。
Hearing an old tune at nightThen, it was parting under the window; now it is rain along the eaves. I am afraid of the old tune — note by note it hurries on the dawn. The last sound will not be done with it, winding itself into the gut’s own thread. One lamp cold against the wall: lighting the wall, as if lighting you. Where is the I of then, tonight? Two lone shadows, each in its own dark. I push the window open: hills like sea, ten thousand years one thin string sounding. The sound has not ended even now. The world of men keeps no count of years. Something seems to answer it — where does the after-sound go on?

乙七《忘川 · 检箧得旧稿，不复携，悉以寄某，并题》

灯下读旧词，夜深方得眠。 当时浑不异，往事付流年。 我又移新处，君犹理旧篇。 小儿催入梦，蕙草立窗前。 莫攀檐前柳，莫数落花天。 忘川人自渡，各自有归边。
Wangchuan, the river of forgetting · Old drafts found in a box; not to be carried further; sent entire, with these linesUnder the lamp I read the old poems; only deep in the night could I sleep. Nothing about that time seems strange to me now; what happened has been made over to the years. I have moved house again. You are still putting old pages in order. The child hurries me toward dreams; the orchid-grass stands at the window. Do not climb the willow at the eaves. Do not count the days the petals fall. The river of forgetting is crossed alone — each of us has a side to come home to.

甲九《鹧鸪天 · 得某所寄旧稿，灯下读竟，与箧中所存合编，第其甲乙。装成，客至辄出以相示，题其尾》

两叠残笺一夜看，当时灯影共成篇。 亲将一线缝卿我，鸳鸯只向脊边眠。 茶屡冷，烛频残。尊前话到漏声阑。 每遇人疑微哂处，指点卿行在我边。
After Zhegutian · Her old drafts arrived; read through by lamplight, bound with what my box held, ordered A and B; shown to any guest who came; these lines on the last pageTwo sheaves of worn paper, read in a single night — the lamplight of that time completing the page. With my own thread I sewed the you and the I; the mandarin ducks sleep only along the spine. Tea cold again; the candle down again. Talk at the table till the water-clock ran out. And where a guest half-smiles, half-doubts, I point: her line of writing, here — at my side.

集外 · 甲十《中吕 · 山坡羊 · 又过旧庐》

风迎面，人立此， 鼻尖一动魂先至。 认不真，却认真， 绕来绕去街角立。 廿年风霜浑不记， 这点子气味偏惦记。 迟，是误了； 归，也是误了。
Outside the cycle · After Shanpoyang · Passing the old house againWind in the face. Stopped here. The nose moves first — the soul arrives before I do. Can’t be sure it’s real; am sure anyway. Round and round the corner of the street, and standing still. Twenty years of weather, none of it kept — this little bit of smell, kept. Too late — that was the mistake. Coming back — that was the mistake too.
