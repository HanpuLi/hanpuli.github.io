Film research

Public dataset · October 2026

First Love (2019): frame-accurate shot-length measurements

Independent analysis of a 147.88-second betrayal-sequence excerpt from Takashi Miike's First Love, identifying 17 complete consecutive shots (mean shot length 8.70 seconds). Cut candidates were generated computationally and checked against adjacent video frames. The measurement is specific to one FilmFour television recording and is not a full-film statistic.

Dataset, methods and FACT export · Merged data contribution · Cinemetrics database entry

Database correction pending: the current Cinemetrics summary counts one incomplete boundary shot and therefore displays 18 shots, 150.9 seconds and ASL 8.4 seconds.
