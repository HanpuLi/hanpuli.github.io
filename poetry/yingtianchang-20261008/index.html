《應天長·喚回》

喚回猶把空衣理， 袖口餘溫留在指。 掩門初，聲又起， 聽盡無聲偏側耳。 指微蜷，杯倒置， 匙底尚留殘齒。 不許指溫先退， 土木火金水

To the tune of Yingtianchang · Calling Back

Calling back, still I straighten the empty robe; the last warmth at its cuff stays on my fingers. As the door begins to close, the sound rises again; I listen through the silence, yet still turn an ear. A finger curls slightly; the cup lies overturned. Under the spoon, a broken tooth still remains. I will not let the warmth in my fingers go first. Earth, wood, fire, metal, water.
