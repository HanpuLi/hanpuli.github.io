Has a summer come to mind?

Early poem · 2017

Written 6–20 November 2017 · revised 8 October 2026

English translation of the October 2026 revision. Read the Chinese original

Has a summer come to mind?

That summer, July’s cicadas had not fallen silent, and your laughter had not stopped. You sang for me to hear, I wrote poems to recite for you.

Let me go back, I miss those days so much. But how can I take you with me, when you are always so silent?

I loved the sea’s vastness, but overlooked the abrupt rise of mountains. I still run towards the light; even the sun keeps hiding here and there.

Some say there are many stars, but you know, however many there are, none of them is you, after all. I only go where you are.

There, drifting clouds still turn and pass, yet day and night are equal in length. Keep me trapped there, let me remember how you look, moment by moment.

There are the songs you sang there, the lines I read to you; and you, my friend, stay in my thoughts all the more.

Will you remember the silent me?

Back then, the hurt was deep in my throat, but my heart was still warm. I sing hoarsely for you; please do not leave yet.

Time, slow down, even if I sing with the years of my life. I gaze at you like this, through a keyhole, through cloud and mist.

Is it a word, or a phrase? The years ask me: what does this amount to? My answer is not beautiful: I was lucky to meet you then.

I always remember that shaft of green, flickering among the leaves as the sun first rose. As though I had found a treasure, I could not help smiling, and told everyone I met.

I still sing of friendship like a mountain peak, no matter how long the days and nights. Let me savour the beginning again: the way we talked of everything, without a frown.

There are the songs you sang there, the lines I read to you; and you, my friend, stay in my thoughts all the more.

But what is beautiful fears forever, like the flowers in Chang’an District that shook their heads.

Happy birthday.

I wrote this poem for you.

Will you remember me?
