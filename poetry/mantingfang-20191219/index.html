To the tune of Mantingfang · Thinking of Chutan from afar

Ci

First written 2019.12.19 · Revision 2026.10

《滿庭芳 · 遙思楚潭》

風佩初停，雨梳朝蕣，尺書先識眉彎。 一聲低喚，驚散夢中弦。 墨跡新添氣息，微微顫、欲出行間。 相逢處，餘溫未定，人近楚天寬。 辭顏。名已熟，臨唇卻緩，舌底生瀾。 把千里關山，盡斂襟前。 幾月光陰忽薄，回眸際、指過眉山。 從頭認，詩還半闋，雙影壓箋邊。

To the tune of Mantingfang · Thinking of Chutan from afar

The wind’s pendants have just stilled; rain combs the morning hibiscus; letters knew the arch of the brows first. One low call scatters the dream’s strings. Fresh breath enters the ink-traces; faintly trembling, they are about to emerge from between the lines. At our meeting, the lingering warmth has yet to settle; with you near, the sky of Chu grows wide. Ciyan. A familiar name, yet slow upon the lips; waves rise beneath the tongue. A thousand li of mountains and passes, all gathered at the breast. Months of time grow suddenly thin; in a backward glance, a finger passes over the mountain of a brow. To know you again, from the beginning; the poem still half-written, two shadows press on the edge of the page.
