Cait Mail Assistant

A single-user personal utility for reading, drafting and filing its author’s own email.

overview privacy terms hanpuli.github.io

What it is

Cait Mail Assistant is a private tool built and used by Hanpu Li. It connects a locally installed assistant client to the Gmail accounts it is authorised against, so that correspondence can be searched, read, drafted and filed from one place.

It is not a product. It is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up. The only accounts it is ever authorised against are the author’s own, and those of a member of her household with that person’s permission.

Google data it uses

gmail.modify
Read messages, threads, labels and attachments; compose, amend and send messages and drafts; apply and remove labels; move messages to and from the trash. It does not permit permanent deletion bypassing the trash.
gmail.settings.basic
Read and adjust basic mailbox settings such as filters and signatures.

Both are classified by Google as restricted scopes. No other Google service is accessed, and the tool holds no billing, contacts, calendar, file or location permissions.

Limited Use

Cait Mail Assistant’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the tool does with that data, and who else sees it, is set out in the privacy policy.
