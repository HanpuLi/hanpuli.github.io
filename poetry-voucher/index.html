Voucher

A poem, issued on receipt paper.

A two-part edition: a fictional till receipt, followed by a voucher carrying the poem. Choose a published work or set your own text in a format made for 58 mm thermal paper.

01

From transaction to reading

The receipt records items, a simulated payment and change. A shared reference joins it to the poem below. Pricing follows a visible fictional tariff, not a judgement of literary merit.

02

Made on your device

Typesetting and PDF / PNG creation happen in your browser. Your text is not uploaded or automatically saved. There is no payment processing and no connection to a remote printer.

03

A physical constraint

Designed around the H10S: 58 mm paper, 384 printable dots, one-bit black and white. These are digital proofs; the physical print still needs calibration. The public site contains no device identifiers or private printing endpoints.
