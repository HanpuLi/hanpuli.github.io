Poetry
Voucher

A poem, issued on receipt paper.

Poems written before this project are offered by the piece, with a base specification and optional extras. Poetry Voucher places individual writing inside the familiar routines of a supermarket purchase.

Enter the shopRead the poems

01

Why a shop

Poems are slow, personal writing: they take time, attention and knowledge, and they are not interchangeable. The shop does the opposite. It treats each poem as a unit on a shelf, gives it a base specification and lets a till calculate a price. The project began after I found a receipt printer and tried printing my poems on it. The supermarket receipt became a way to ask what changes when private writing is issued through the routines of an ordinary commodity.

02

How the price works

The fictional tariff deliberately does not measure writing time, difficulty or literary value. It reduces a poem to countable features of its source text: characters, non-empty lines and stanzas. A base amount is rounded upward to a retail .99 price point. Website typefaces and each added translation are optional extras. The typeface works like a product upgrade from the included default specification; translations also use additional paper and printing, while carrying the work into another language and cultural context. No money is taken.

03

Receipt, voucher, paper

An order is issued as one continuous 58 mm strip: the receipt first, then CUT HERE, then the numbered poem voucher or vouchers. The receipt records the configured transaction; the voucher carries the writing away from it. Cutting is offered rather than required. Thermal paper keeps the scale and handling of an everyday receipt: something that can sit in a pocket, be passed to someone else, and, materially, can fade with time. Fading is a property of the medium, not yet a timed claim made by the work.

04

Digital access and physical form

The browser shop is the public access route while no exhibition space is available. It generates a continuous PDF and a complete offline-readable text counterpart without taking payment or sending the poem to a server. Physical prints have also been made on the receipt printer. The intended material encounter remains the narrow thermal strip; the digital shop makes that edition accessible beyond a physical venue.

05

Position

Poetry Voucher does not claim that receipt printing makes poetry new, nor that its tariff is a fair model of creative labour. Its tension is narrower: writing that is personal and non-interchangeable is temporarily handled as stock, configuration and price. The retail system can count textual units, paper and options, but it cannot account for the time, knowledge, attachment or relation that made the poem. The work leaves the reader to decide what happens after that transaction: keep the strip, cut it, give the poem away, or discard it.
